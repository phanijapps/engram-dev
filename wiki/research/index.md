# Domain

- [EPIC — 40 practices for specification-driven development artifacts (arXiv 2610.07534)](epic-framework.md) - Claudino and others, 2026. An empirical study of 114 open-source SDD repositories. 40 practices in 10 quality dimensions in 4 categories: Expressive, Precise, Interpretable, Compartmentalized. Better specifications tracked with fewer bug-fix commits and more contributors.
- [Misfit-Governed Development — a design theory for AI-assisted software (arXiv 2610.01372)](misfit-governed-development.md) - Chen and Cheng, 2026. Fit is seen only through misfits. Verification is an enumerated, machine-checked misfit list. A dual loop separates machine-paced repair from human-paced judgment. The builder may never amend the law.
- [The SDD loop synthesis — three research lines, one loop](sdd-loop-synthesis.md) - What three 2026 papers agree on, where they tension, and what our harness loop should adopt. Arming the loop redesign (proposals P-1 to P-5, pending owner decisions Q1/Q2).
- [SpecAgent — agentic synthesis of formal specifications (arXiv 2610.05132)](specagent.md) - 2026. Specification synthesis for C program verification. Forward-only workflows with localized repair fail: weak upstream specifications cause cascading downstream failures. SpecAgent traces root causes across dependencies, revisits finished specifications, and critiques strength beyond proof success.
