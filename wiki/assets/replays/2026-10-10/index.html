07 · Policy versus enforcement

Human acceptance

Answers, approval, decision acceptance, and closure require a human: claim.

The claim checks a string prefix. It does not authenticate a person.

Recorded evidence

A passing task needs at least one recorded command and zero exits. Evidence can come from an agent.

do record accepts supplied results. It does not execute the command.

Closure and review

Closure checks tasks, evidence presence, decisions, questions, and a PACK PASS verdict.

do check checks alignment. The review skill requires the actual re-runs.

Documentation overstates the scope of the claim gate.

“Every write needs human:” does not match proposal creation, task handouts, evidence records, or knowledge-ledger resolution.

A standing view of the records
